# Project Joy: UI/UX Design Brief &amp; Brand Identity

## 1. Visual Identity &amp; Aesthetic Principles
- **Design Philosophy:** Minimalist, high-end "digital workforce" management studio. Clean, quiet, and supremely functional.
- **Strict Color Policy:** **Zero blue and zero purple.** Strictly avoid the generic dark-purple "vibe-coded AI app" aesthetic. The entire UI uses a pristine light canvas with purposeful, organic commerce accents.
- **Color Palette &amp; Tokens:**
  - **Canvas &amp; Surfaces:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) primary background, Warm Stone Light (`#FAFAF9`) secondary canvas, Soft Border Muted Gray (`#E4E4E7`).
  - **Typography &amp; Structure:** Rich Charcoal / Deep Onyx (`#18181B`) for crisp, high-legibility headings and body text; Slate Muted (`#71717A`) for secondary captions.
  - **Brand &amp; Commerce Primary:** Deep Emerald Forest (`#059669`) / Hunter Green (`#047857`) representing active growth, business revenue, and trust.
  - **Operational Status Accents:**
    - Active Employee: Emerald Pulse Badge (`#10B981` bg with `#065F46` text).
    - Pending / Needs Attention: Warm Amber (`#D97706`).
    - Human Takeover Required (HITL Alert): Clean Crimson (`#E11D48`).
- **Typography:** Modern Swiss-style geometric typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Geist Sans`), high readability at 14px/16px, large friendly metrics, generous whitespace, and zero visual clutter.

## 2. Localization &amp; Non-Technical Usability (Plug-and-Play)
- **Target Audience Context:** Tailored for non-technical retail and e-commerce business owners (e.g., merchants in Dhaka, Bangladesh and emerging global commerce hubs) who need tools that are as simple as WhatsApp or bKash.
- **Zero Jargon Rule:** Eliminate technical AI terminology from the UI:
  - *No "RAG", "pgvector", or "Embeddings"* &rarr; Label as **"Employee Training"** or **"Store Knowledge"**.
  - *No "LLM Parameters", "Temperature", or "System Prompt"* &rarr; Label as **"Employee Instructions &amp; Tone"**.
  - *No "Webhook Ingestion"* &rarr; Label as **"Store Connections"**.
- **Metaphor — "Hiring Digital Staff":** The dashboard presents agents as tangible, full-time digital employees ("Social Sales Specialist", "Order Confirmation Specialist") that merchants can hire, test, monitor, and scale with 1-click simplicity.

## 3. Core Dashboard Architecture (4 Clean Views)

### Page 1: Store Connections (Integration Hub)
- **Status Overview:** Clean white cards showing live connection health for Facebook/Instagram, Shopify, WooCommerce, and Website Chat.
- **Meta Quick Connect:** Single prominent green button: *"Connect Facebook &amp; Instagram"* launching the 1-click Meta Embedded Signup popup.
- **Storefront Binding:** Simplified Shopify OAuth and WooCommerce API key input cards with clear green "Connected" toggles.
- **Website Chat Snippet:** Interactive clean white widget preview with a 1-click *"Copy Embed Code"* button.

### Page 2: Employee Training (Knowledge Brain)
- **Catalog Uploader:** Large, friendly drag-and-drop zone accepting PDF catalogs, product spreadsheets (CSV), or price lists.
- **Website Importer:** Simple URL input: *"Paste your website link"* with a green *"Import Products"* button.
- **Knowledge List:** Clean status table showing indexed products and training documents with simple green checkmarks.

### Page 3: Digital Employee Roster
- **Employee Cards:** Visual staff profile cards for **Employee #1 (Social Sales Specialist)** and **Employee #2 (Phone Confirmation Specialist)** with their active status and monthly revenue generated.
- **Simple Controls:** Friendly sliders for conversation style (Friendly vs. Formal), discount limits (e.g., maximum 10%), working hours, and phone call hours.
- **Roster Expansion:** Clean `+ Hire New Employee` button to add upcoming digital workers (e.g., Returns &amp; Exchange Specialist, Cart Recovery Specialist).

### Page 4: Operator Desk (Unified Inbox &amp; Human Takeover)
- **Clean Message Stream:** Unified conversation list filtered by channel (Instagram, Messenger, Web Chat, Phone Call).
- **Sentiment &amp; Risk Alert:** Subtle, clear Green / Yellow / Red indicators.
- **Live Conversation &amp; Draft Editor:** Displays the live conversation, the employee's pending response draft, and a prominent 1-click toggle: **"Take Over Conversation"** (Crimson button) to pause the employee and chat directly with the customer.
